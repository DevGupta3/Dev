PROJECT 01

Sales Data Analysis & Interactive Dashboard

Sales Data Analysis & Interactive Dashboard Project Description: Analyzed a retail sales dataset to identify sales trends, customer behavior, product performance, regional performance, and profitability. Created an interactive Power BI dashboard to present key business insights in an easy-to-understand visual format. Analysis Process: Excel: Cleaned and formatted raw sales data, removed duplicates, handled missing values, and performed initial data analysis using formulas, Pivot Tables, and charts. Python: Used Pandas and NumPy for data cleaning, transformation, aggregation, and exploratory data analysis. Created visualizations using Matplotlib and Seaborn to identify important patterns and trends. Power BI: Built an interactive dashboard with KPI cards, filters, slicers, charts, and drill-down analysis for Sales, Profit, Orders, Customers, Categories, Regions, Products, and Segments. Key Findings: Identified the highest-performing product categories and sub-categories. Analyzed monthly and yearly sales trends to identify peak sales periods. Compared regional and state-wise sales and profit performance. Identified top-selling and most profitable products. Analyzed customer segments and their contribution to overall revenue. Evaluated discount impact on sales and profitability. Identified areas with high sales but comparatively low profit for further business improvement. Tools Used: Microsoft Excel | Python | Pandas | NumPy | Matplotlib | Seaborn | Power BI
